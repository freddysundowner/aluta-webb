Our Origins

Rooted in Kenya.
Reaching everywhere.

Incorporated in Nakuru in 2019, Aluta Technology Ventures Limited was founded by Fredrick Mundia Githumbi with a simple premise: African businesses deserve world-class software that understands their unique operational realities.

We don't just build apps — we build ecosystems. From helping a local retailer manage inventory to enabling developers to integrate seamless payments, our suite of tools is the digital infrastructure for the next generation of African enterprise.
